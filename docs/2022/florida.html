Florida - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Florida, nonprofits received a total of $15.0B in government grants. Nonprofits in Miami-Dade, Hillsborough, and Broward Counties and the 27th, 14th, and 2nd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Taylor, Jackson, and Santa Rosa Counties and the 12th, 2nd, and 9th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Bradford, Calhoun, and DeSoto Counties and the 2nd, 28th, and 1st congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
