Alabama - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Alabama, nonprofits received a total of $2.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Jefferson, Montgomery, and Mobile Counties and the 7th, 2nd, and 3rd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Lamar, Pickens, and Talladega Counties and the 3rd, 7th, and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Bibb, Bullock, and Butler Counties and the 4th, 7th, and 3rd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
