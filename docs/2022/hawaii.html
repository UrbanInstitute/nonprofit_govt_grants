Hawaii - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Hawaii, nonprofits received a total of $1.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Honolulu, Maui, and Hawaii Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Maui, Honolulu, and Hawaii Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Maui, Hawaii, and Honolulu Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
