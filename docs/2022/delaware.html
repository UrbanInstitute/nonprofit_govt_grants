Delaware - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Delaware, nonprofits received a total of $1.3B in government grants. Nonprofits in New Castle, Kent, and Sussex Counties received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Kent, New Castle, and Sussex Counties would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Kent, New Castle, and Sussex Counties have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
