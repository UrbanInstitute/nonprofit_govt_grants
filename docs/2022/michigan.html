Michigan - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Michigan, nonprofits received a total of $5.2B in government grants. Nonprofits in Wayne, Washtenaw, and Kent Counties and the 13th, 6th, and 3rd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Luce, Iron, and Presque Isle Counties and the 10th, 11th, and 13th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Alger, Antrim, and Arenac Counties and the 10th, 7th, and 13th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
