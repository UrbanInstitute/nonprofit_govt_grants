Arizona - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Arizona, nonprofits received a total of $4.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Maricopa, Pima, and Coconino Counties and the 3rd, 7th, and 4th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Graham, Mohave, and Pinal Counties and the 5th, 9th, and 3rd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Pinal, Graham, and Cochise Counties and the 5th, 3rd, and 7th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
