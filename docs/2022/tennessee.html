Tennessee - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Tennessee, nonprofits received a total of $3.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Davidson, Shelby, and Knox Counties and the 7th, 9th, and 2nd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Weakley, Haywood, and Sequatchie Counties and the 9th, 1st, and 4th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Benton, Decatur, and Gibson Counties and the 9th, 1st, and 6th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
