Nebraska - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Nebraska, nonprofits received a total of $988.1M in government grants. Nonprofits in Douglas, Lancaster, and Sarpy Counties and the 2nd, 1st, and 3rd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Sheridan, Cherry, and Cuming Counties and the 3rd, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Boone, Cass, and Cedar Counties and the 3rd, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
