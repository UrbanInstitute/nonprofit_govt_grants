Washington - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Washington, nonprofits received a total of $6.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in King, Pierce, and Spokane Counties and the 7th, 9th, and 4th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Pend Oreille, Whitman, and Klickitat Counties and the 9th, 5th, and 10th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Adams, Lincoln, and Pend Oreille Counties and the 9th, 5th, and 10th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
