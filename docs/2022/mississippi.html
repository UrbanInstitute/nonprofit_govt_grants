Mississippi - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Mississippi, nonprofits received a total of $997.5M in government grants. Nonprofits in Hinds, Washington, and Rankin Counties and the 2nd, 3rd, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Noxubee, Tallahatchie, and Winston Counties and the 4th, 2nd, and 3rd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Adams, Alcorn, and Amite Counties and the 3rd, 4th, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
