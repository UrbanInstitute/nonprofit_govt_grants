New York - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In New York, nonprofits received a total of $30.5B in government grants. Nonprofits in New York, Kings, and Bronx Counties and the 10th, 12th, and 13th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Tioga, Wyoming, and Orleans Counties and the 8th, 5th, and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Putnam, Wyoming, and Chautauqua Counties and the 5th, 8th, and 15th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
