Kentucky - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Kentucky, nonprofits received a total of $2.3B in government grants. Nonprofits in Jefferson, Fayette, and Franklin Counties and the 3rd, 6th, and 5th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Breathitt, Morgan, and Fulton Counties and the 5th, 1st, and 3rd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Bracken, Caldwell, and Carter Counties and the 5th, 4th, and 1st congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
