Connecticut - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Connecticut, nonprofits received a total of $4.2B in government grants. Nonprofits in South Central Connecticut Planning Region, Capitol Planning Region, and Greater Bridgeport Planning Region Counties and the 3rd, 4th, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, South Central Connecticut Planning Region, and Greater Bridgeport Planning Region Counties and the 1st, 3rd, and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Greater Bridgeport Planning Region, South Central Connecticut Planning Region, and Capitol Planning Region Counties and the 1st, 3rd, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
