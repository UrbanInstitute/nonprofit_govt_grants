United States - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In the United States, nonprofits received a total of $294.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in California, New York, and Ohio received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Louisiana, Mississippi, and Delaware would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Alaska, Delaware, and Mississippi have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
