Louisiana - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Louisiana, nonprofits received a total of $3.0B in government grants. Nonprofits in Orleans, East Baton Rouge, and Jefferson Counties and the 2nd, 1st, and 6th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Concordia, Grant, and LaSalle Counties and the 5th, 3rd, and 6th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Allen, Ascension, and Assumption Counties and the 6th, 5th, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
