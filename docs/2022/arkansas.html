Arkansas - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Arkansas, nonprofits received a total of $1.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Pulaski, Sebastian, and Washington Counties and the 2nd, 1st, and 3rd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Pike, Dallas, and Lee Counties and the 1st, 2nd, and 4th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Ashley, Boone, and Bradley Counties and the 4th, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
