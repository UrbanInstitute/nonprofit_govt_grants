West Virginia - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In West Virginia, nonprofits received a total of $1.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Kanawha, Monongalia, and Cabell Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Gilmer, McDowell, and Monroe Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Clay, Doddridge, and Gilmer Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
