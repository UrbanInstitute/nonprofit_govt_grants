Maryland - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Maryland, nonprofits received a total of $6.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Baltimore, Montgomery, and Prince George’s Counties and the 2nd, 8th, and 7th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Somerset, Dorchester, and Worcester Counties and the 5th and 1st congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Somerset, Charles, and Allegany Counties and the 1st and 5th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
