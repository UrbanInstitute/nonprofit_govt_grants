South Dakota - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In South Dakota, nonprofits received a total of $648.4M in government grants. Nonprofits in Pennington, Minnehaha, and Jackson Counties received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Deuel, Corson, and Todd Counties would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Bennett, Clay, and Corson Counties have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
