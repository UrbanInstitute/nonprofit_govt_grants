Vermont - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Vermont, nonprofits received a total of $659.3M in government grants. Nonprofits in Chittenden, Washington, and Windham Counties received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Rutland, Orange, and Franklin Counties would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Rutland, Chittenden, and Orange Counties have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
