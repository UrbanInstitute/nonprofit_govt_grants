Missouri - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Missouri, nonprofits received a total of $4.0B in government grants. Nonprofits in St. Louis, Jackson, and St. Louis Counties and the 1st, 5th, and 7th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in St. Clair, Schuyler, and Perry Counties and the 4th, 8th, and 1st congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Andrew, Benton, and Bollinger Counties and the 4th, 1st, and 8th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
