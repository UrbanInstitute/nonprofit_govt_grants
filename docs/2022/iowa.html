Iowa - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Iowa, nonprofits received a total of $1.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Polk, Linn, and Black Hawk Counties and the 3rd, 2nd, and 4th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Adams, Osceola, and Clay Counties and the 3rd, 4th, and 1st congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Adams, Buchanan, and Buena Vista Counties and the 3rd, 1st, and 4th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
