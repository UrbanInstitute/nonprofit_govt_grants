Pennsylvania - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Pennsylvania, nonprofits received a total of $12.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Philadelphia, Allegheny, and Dauphin Counties and the 3rd, 12th, and 2nd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Lawrence, Monroe, and Bedford Counties and the 16th, 1st, and 13th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Cameron, Forest, and Lycoming Counties and the 16th, 1st, and 4th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
