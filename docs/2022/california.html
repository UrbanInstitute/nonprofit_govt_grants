California - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In California, nonprofits received a total of $49.0B in government grants. Nonprofits in Los Angeles, San Diego, and Alameda Counties and the 11th, 37th, and 12th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Sutter, Yuba, and Lassen Counties and the 31st, 23rd, and 27th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Alpine, Inyo, and Lassen Counties and the 1st, 31st, and 43rd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
