Texas - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Texas, nonprofits received a total of $16.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Harris, Bexar, and Travis Counties and the 9th, 35th, and 20th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Jackson, Rains, and Hartley Counties and the 15th, 29th, and 16th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Andrews, Bailey, and Calhoun Counties and the 34th, 35th, and 20th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
