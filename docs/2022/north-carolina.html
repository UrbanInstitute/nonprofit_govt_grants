North Carolina - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In North Carolina, nonprofits received a total of $7.6B in government grants. Nonprofits in Durham, Wake, and Mecklenburg Counties and the 4th, 2nd, and 10th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Hoke, Northampton, and Wayne Counties and the 1st, 3rd, and 9th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Anson, Caswell, and Gates Counties and the 12th, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
