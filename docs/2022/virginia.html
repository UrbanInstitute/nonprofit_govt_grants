Virginia - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Virginia, nonprofits received a total of $5.0B in government grants. Nonprofits in Arlington, Fairfax, and Alexandria Counties and the 8th, 11th, and 9th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Charles City, Covington, and Cumberland Counties and the 7th, 5th, and 9th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Amelia, Bland, and Charles City Counties and the 7th, 9th, and 3rd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
