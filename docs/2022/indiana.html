Indiana - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Indiana, nonprofits received a total of $3.2B in government grants. Nonprofits in Marion, Lake, and St. Joseph Counties and the 7th, 2nd, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Randolph, Sullivan, and Martin Counties and the 1st, 6th, and 9th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Blackford, Carroll, and Clinton Counties and the 1st, 4th, and 5th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
