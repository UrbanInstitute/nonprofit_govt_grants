Colorado - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Colorado, nonprofits received a total of $3.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Denver, Jefferson, and Boulder Counties and the 1st, 7th, and 2nd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Gilpin, Crowley, and Clear Creek Counties and the 1st, 8th, and 6th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Conejos, Costilla, and Crowley Counties and the 1st, 8th, and 4th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
