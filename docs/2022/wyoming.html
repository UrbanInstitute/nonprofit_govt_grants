Wyoming - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2022

In Wyoming, nonprofits received a total of $284.7M in government grants. Nonprofits in Sheridan, Laramie, and Natrona Counties received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Platte, Converse, and Uinta Counties would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Converse, Crook, and Lincoln Counties have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
