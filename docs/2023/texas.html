Texas - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Texas, nonprofits received a total of $13.3B in government grants. Nonprofits in Bexar, Harris, and Travis Counties and the 35th, 21st, and 9th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Upshur, Jackson, and Newton Counties and the 5th, 6th, and 15th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Anderson, Andrews, and Atascosa Counties and the 14th, 6th, and 16th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
