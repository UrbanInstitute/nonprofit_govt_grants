Vermont - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Vermont, nonprofits received a total of $560.7M in government grants. Nonprofits in Chittenden, Washington, and Rutland Counties received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Rutland, Grand Isle, and Washington Counties would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Orange, Rutland, and Washington Counties have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
