Georgia - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Georgia, nonprofits received a total of $4.7B in government grants. Nonprofits in Fulton, DeKalb, and Clarke Counties and the 5th, 2nd, and 10th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Macon, Hancock, and Banks Counties and the 13th, 12th, and 14th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Appling, Bacon, and Baker Counties and the 13th, 12th, and 10th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
