Alaska - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Alaska, nonprofits received a total of $1.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Anchorage, Nome, and Juneau Counties received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Copper River, Bethel, and Lake and Peninsula Counties would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Aleutians West, Bethel, and Bristol Bay Counties have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
