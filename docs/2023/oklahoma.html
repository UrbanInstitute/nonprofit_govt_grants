Oklahoma - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Oklahoma, nonprofits received a total of $1.2B in government grants. Nonprofits in Oklahoma, Tulsa, and Bryan Counties and the 5th, 1st, and 3rd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Coal, Dewey, and Nowata Counties and the 2nd, 5th, and 3rd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Alfalfa, Beckham, and Caddo Counties and the 5th, 2nd, and 3rd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
