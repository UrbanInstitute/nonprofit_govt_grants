Wisconsin - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Wisconsin, nonprofits received a total of $2.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Milwaukee, Dane, and Fond du Lac Counties and the 4th, 2nd, and 6th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Menominee, Rusk, and Douglas Counties and the 4th, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Crawford, Iron, and Menominee Counties and the 4th, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
