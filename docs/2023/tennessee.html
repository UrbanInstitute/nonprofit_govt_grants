Tennessee - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Tennessee, nonprofits received a total of $3.3B in government grants. Nonprofits in Davidson, Shelby, and Knox Counties and the 7th, 9th, and 3rd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Haywood, Lauderdale, and Moore Counties and the 9th, 6th, and 1st congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Bedford, Benton, and Cannon Counties and the 9th, 7th, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
