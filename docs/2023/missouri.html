Missouri - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Missouri, nonprofits received a total of $2.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Jackson, St. Louis, and St. Louis Counties and the 1st, 5th, and 8th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in New Madrid, Bates, and Wayne Counties and the 4th, 8th, and 3rd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Barton, Bates, and Benton Counties and the 4th, 5th, and 3rd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
