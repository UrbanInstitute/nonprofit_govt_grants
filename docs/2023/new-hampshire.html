New Hampshire - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In New Hampshire, nonprofits received a total of $714.2M in government grants. Nonprofits in Hillsborough, Grafton, and Merrimack Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Hillsborough, Sullivan, and Coos Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Cheshire, Coos, and Hillsborough Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
