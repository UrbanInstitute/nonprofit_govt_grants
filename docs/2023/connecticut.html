Connecticut - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Connecticut, nonprofits received a total of $3.7B in government grants. Nonprofits in South Central Connecticut Planning Region, Greater Bridgeport Planning Region, and Capitol Planning Region Counties and the 3rd, 4th, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, Greater Bridgeport Planning Region, and Naugatuck Valley Planning Region Counties and the 1st, 3rd, and 5th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, Naugatuck Valley Planning Region, and Capitol Planning Region Counties and the 1st, 3rd, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
