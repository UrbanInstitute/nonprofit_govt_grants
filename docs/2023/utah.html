Utah - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Utah, nonprofits received a total of $1.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Salt Lake, Utah, and Weber Counties and the 2nd, 3rd, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Iron, Duchesne, and Carbon Counties and the 4th, 2nd, and 1st congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Duchesne, Emery, and Iron Counties and the 2nd, 3rd, and 1st congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
