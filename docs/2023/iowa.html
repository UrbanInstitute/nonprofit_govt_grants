Iowa - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Iowa, nonprofits received a total of $998.3M in government grants. Nonprofits in Polk, Linn, and Scott Counties and the 3rd, 2nd, and 4th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Adams, Buchanan, and Cerro Gordo Counties and the 3rd, 1st, and 4th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Adair, Adams, and Buchanan Counties and the 3rd, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
