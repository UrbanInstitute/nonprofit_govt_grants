North Carolina - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In North Carolina, nonprofits received a total of $6.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Durham, Wake, and Forsyth Counties and the 4th, 2nd, and 10th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Alexander, Hoke, and Granville Counties and the 3rd, 8th, and 13th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Anson, Caswell, and Currituck Counties and the 3rd, 13th, and 10th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
