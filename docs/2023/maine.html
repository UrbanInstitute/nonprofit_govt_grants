Maine - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Maine, nonprofits received a total of $970.2M in government grants. Nonprofits in Cumberland, Kennebec, and Hancock Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Kennebec, Aroostook, and Androscoggin Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Androscoggin, Washington, and Kennebec Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
