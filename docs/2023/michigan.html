Michigan - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Michigan, nonprofits received a total of $4.7B in government grants. Nonprofits in Wayne, Washtenaw, and Ingham Counties and the 6th, 13th, and 7th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Iron, Iosco, and Clare Counties and the 10th, 13th, and 11th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Arenac, Crawford, and Dickinson Counties and the 12th, 7th, and 10th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
