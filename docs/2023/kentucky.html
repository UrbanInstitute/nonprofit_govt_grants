Kentucky - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Kentucky, nonprofits received a total of $1.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Jefferson, Fayette, and Franklin Counties and the 3rd, 5th, and 6th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Lawrence, Breathitt, and Morgan Counties and the 5th, 4th, and 6th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Anderson, Bell, and Boyd Counties and the 5th, 3rd, and 6th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
