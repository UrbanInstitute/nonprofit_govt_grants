Massachusetts - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Massachusetts, nonprofits received a total of $12.0B in government grants. Nonprofits in Middlesex, Suffolk, and Essex Counties and the 7th, 6th, and 8th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Franklin, Hampshire, and Suffolk Counties and the 8th, 7th, and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Franklin, Hampshire, and Worcester Counties and the 2nd, 3rd, and 7th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
