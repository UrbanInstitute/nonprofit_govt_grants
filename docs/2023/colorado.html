Colorado - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Colorado, nonprofits received a total of $2.9B in government grants. Nonprofits in Denver, Boulder, and El Paso Counties and the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Park, Crowley, and Jackson Counties and the 1st, 7th, and 6th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Conejos, Crowley, and Dolores Counties and the 1st, 7th, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
