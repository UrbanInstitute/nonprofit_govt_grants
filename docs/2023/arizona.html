Arizona - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Arizona, nonprofits received a total of $3.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Maricopa, Pima, and Coconino Counties and the 3rd, 7th, and 4th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Apache, Mohave, and Graham Counties and the 5th, 9th, and 7th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Pinal, Santa Cruz, and Coconino Counties and the 5th, 7th, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
