New Jersey - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In New Jersey, nonprofits received a total of $5.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Essex, Mercer, and Middlesex Counties and the 10th, 12th, and 6th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Hudson, Warren, and Passaic Counties and the 10th, 9th, and 8th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Hudson, Warren, and Essex Counties and the 10th, 8th, and 1st congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
