Hawaii - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Hawaii, nonprofits received a total of $771.1M in government grants. Nonprofits in Honolulu, Maui, and Hawaii Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Honolulu, Hawaii, and Maui Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Honolulu, Hawaii, and Kauai Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
