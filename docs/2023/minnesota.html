Minnesota - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Minnesota, nonprofits received a total of $3.9B in government grants. Nonprofits in Hennepin, Ramsey, and Olmsted Counties and the 4th, 5th, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Red Lake, Traverse, and Kanabec Counties and the 4th, 5th, and 8th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Big Stone, Cottonwood, and Dodge Counties and the 4th, 6th, and 5th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
