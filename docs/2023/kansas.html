Kansas - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Kansas, nonprofits received a total of $903.3M in government grants. Nonprofits in Sedgwick, Shawnee, and Douglas Counties and the 1st, 2nd, and 4th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Chase, Sheridan, and Clay Counties and the 2nd, 1st, and 4th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Anderson, Atchison, and Bourbon Counties and the 2nd, 1st, and 4th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
