Nevada - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Nevada, nonprofits received a total of $1.3B in government grants. Nonprofits in Clark, Washoe, and Carson City Counties and the 2nd, 4th, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Churchill, White Pine, and Nye Counties and the 3rd, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Humboldt, Lyon, and Mineral Counties and the 1st, 4th, and 3rd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
