Pennsylvania - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Pennsylvania, nonprofits received a total of $8.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Philadelphia, Allegheny, and Dauphin Counties and the 3rd, 10th, and 16th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Lawrence, McKean, and Greene Counties and the 16th, 17th, and 12th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Lawrence, Pike, and Erie Counties and the 12th, 4th, and 16th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
