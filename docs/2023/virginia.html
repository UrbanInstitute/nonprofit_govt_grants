Virginia - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Virginia, nonprofits received a total of $4.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Arlington, Fairfax, and Alexandria Counties and the 8th, 11th, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Cumberland, King and Queen, and King William Counties and the 7th, 1st, and 9th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Amelia, Bland, and Buckingham Counties and the 7th, 3rd, and 5th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
