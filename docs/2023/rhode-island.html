Rhode Island - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Rhode Island, nonprofits received a total of $625.1M in government grants. Nonprofits in Providence, Kent, and Washington Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Providence, Kent, and Bristol Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Providence, Kent, and Newport Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
