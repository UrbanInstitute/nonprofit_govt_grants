Indiana - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In Indiana, nonprofits received a total of $2.7B in government grants. Nonprofits in Marion, Elkhart, and St. Joseph Counties and the 7th, 2nd, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Pike, Randolph, and Newton Counties and the 1st, 6th, and 4th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Blackford, Carroll, and Crawford Counties and the 4th, 1st, and 6th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
