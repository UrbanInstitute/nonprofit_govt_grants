New York - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In New York, nonprofits received a total of $24.0B in government grants. Nonprofits in New York, Kings, and Albany Counties and the 12th, 10th, and 20th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Orleans, Greene, and Cayuga Counties and the 8th, 5th, and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Cayuga, Wyoming, and Bronx Counties and the 8th, 15th, and 16th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
