California - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2023

In California, nonprofits received a total of $39.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Los Angeles, San Diego, and Alameda Counties and the 12th, 34th, and 15th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Lassen, Yuba, and Sierra Counties and the 23rd, 31st, and 42nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Alpine, Colusa, and Del Norte Counties and the 44th, 42nd, and 33rd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
