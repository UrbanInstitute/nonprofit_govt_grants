California - How Losing Government Grants Would Affect Nonprofit Finances

In California, nonprofits received a total of $45.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Los Angeles, San Diego, and Alameda counties and 11th, 37th, and 12th Congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Colusa, Sierra, and Sutter counties and 42nd, 1st, and 6th Congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Colusa, Lassen, and Sierra counties and 42nd, 1st, and 22nd Congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
