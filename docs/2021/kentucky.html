Kentucky - How Losing Government Grants Would Affect Nonprofit Finances

In Kentucky, nonprofits received a total of $2.5B in government grants. Nonprofits in Jefferson, Fayette, and Franklin counties and 3rd, 6th, and 5th Congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Grayson, Fleming, and Morgan counties and 5th, 4th, and 1st Congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Adair, Ballard, and Bracken counties and 5th, 1st, and 4th Congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
