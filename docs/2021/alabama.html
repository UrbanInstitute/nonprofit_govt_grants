Alabama - How Losing Government Grants Would Affect Nonprofit Finances

In Alabama, nonprofits received a total of $2.3B in government grants. Nonprofits in Jefferson, Montgomery, and Mobile counties and 7th, 2nd, and 5th Congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Pickens, Sumter, and Talladega counties and 3rd, 4th, and 2nd Congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Bullock, Butler, and DeKalb counties and 2nd, 4th, and 3rd Congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
