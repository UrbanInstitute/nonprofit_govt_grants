Virginia - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In Virginia, nonprofits received a total of $5.3B in government grants. Nonprofits in Arlington, Fairfax, and Alexandria Counties and the 8th, 11th, and 9th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Amelia, Covington, and New Kent Counties and the 9th, 7th, and 3rd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Amelia, Bland, and Buena Vista Counties and the 9th, 3rd, and 7th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
