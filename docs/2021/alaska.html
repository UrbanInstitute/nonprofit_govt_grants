Alaska - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In Alaska, nonprofits received a total of $2.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Anchorage, Juneau, and Nome Counties received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Kusilvak, Copper River, and Bethel Counties would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Aleutians West, Bristol Bay, and Copper River Counties have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
