Illinois - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In Illinois, nonprofits received a total of $10.9B in government grants. Nonprofits in Cook, Kane, and McLean Counties and the 7th, 1st, and 17th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Putnam, Pike, and Pulaski Counties and the 12th, 2nd, and 1st congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Alexander, Bond, and Calhoun Counties and the 12th, 1st, and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
