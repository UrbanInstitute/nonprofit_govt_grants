North Carolina - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In North Carolina, nonprofits received a total of $8.9B in government grants. Nonprofits in Durham, Wake, and New Hanover Counties and the 4th, 2nd, and 7th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Anson, Jones, and Northampton Counties and the 1st, 3rd, and 8th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Anson, Gates, and Martin Counties and the 3rd, 7th, and 1st congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
