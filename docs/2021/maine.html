Maine - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In Maine, nonprofits received a total of $1.5B in government grants. Nonprofits in Cumberland, Penobscot, and Androscoggin Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Androscoggin, Kennebec, and Aroostook Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Aroostook, Androscoggin, and Penobscot Counties and the 2nd and 1st congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
