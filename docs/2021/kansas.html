Kansas - How Losing Government Grants Would Affect Nonprofit Finances

In Kansas, nonprofits received a total of $1.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Sedgwick, Douglas, and Shawnee counties and 1st, 2nd, and 4th Congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Morton, Sheridan, and Coffey counties and 2nd, 1st, and 4th Congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Anderson, Barber, and Cheyenne counties and 1st, 2nd, and 4th Congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
