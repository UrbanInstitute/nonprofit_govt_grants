Oklahoma - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In Oklahoma, nonprofits received a total of $1.7B in government grants. Nonprofits in Oklahoma, Tulsa, and Haskell Counties and the 5th, 1st, and 3rd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in McIntosh, Dewey, and Woodward Counties and the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Adair, Alfalfa, and Atoka Counties and the 2nd, 4th, and 3rd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
