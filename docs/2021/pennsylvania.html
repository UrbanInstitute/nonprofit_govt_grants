Pennsylvania - How Losing Government Grants Would Affect Nonprofit Finances

In Pennsylvania, nonprofits received a total of $13.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Philadelphia, Allegheny, and Dauphin counties and 3rd, 12th, and 2nd Congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Cameron, Lawrence, and Pike counties and 16th, 7th, and 8th Congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Cameron, Fulton, and Pike counties and 16th, 7th, and 2nd Congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
