Maryland - How Losing Government Grants Would Affect Nonprofit Finances

In Maryland, nonprofits received a total of $6.4B in government grants. Nonprofits in Baltimore city, Montgomery, and Prince George’s counties and 2nd, 8th, and 7th Congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Somerset, Worcester, and Charles counties would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Somerset, Queen Anne’s, and Allegany counties have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
