Minnesota - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In Minnesota, nonprofits received a total of $4.9B in government grants. Nonprofits in Hennepin, Ramsey, and Olmsted Counties and the 5th, 4th, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Mahnomen, Lac qui Parle, and Grant Counties and the 4th, 8th, and 5th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Grant, Lac qui Parle, and Lake of the Woods Counties and the 4th, 5th, and 6th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
