Texas - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In Texas, nonprofits received a total of $18.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in Bexar, Harris, and Travis Counties and the 21st, 9th, and 35th congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Crosby, Starr, and Cass Counties and the 15th, 28th, and 16th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Andrews, Archer, and Baylor Counties and the 28th, 23rd, and 15th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
