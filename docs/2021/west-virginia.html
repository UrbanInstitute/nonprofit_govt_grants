West Virginia - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In West Virginia, nonprofits received a total of $1.1B in government grants. Nonprofits in Kanawha, Cabell, and Monongalia Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in McDowell, Doddridge, and Gilmer Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Boone, Calhoun, and Doddridge Counties and the 1st and 2nd congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
