Ohio - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In Ohio, nonprofits received a total of $18.0B in government grants. Nonprofits in Franklin, Cuyahoga, and Hamilton Counties and the 3rd, 11th, and 1st congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Marion, Vinton, and Madison Counties and the 3rd, 13th, and 4th congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Harrison, Lawrence, and Morgan Counties and the 3rd, 15th, and 13th congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
