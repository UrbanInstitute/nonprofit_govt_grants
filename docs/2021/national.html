United States - How Losing Government Grants Would Have Affected Nonprofit Finances in 2021

In the United States, nonprofits received a total of $304.8B in government grants. Nonprofits in California, New York, and District of Columbia received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Alaska, Delaware, and West Virginia would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Alaska, West Virginia, and Arkansas have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
