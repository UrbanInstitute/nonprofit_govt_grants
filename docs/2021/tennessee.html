Tennessee - How Losing Government Grants Would Affect Nonprofit Finances

In Tennessee, nonprofits received a total of $3.9B in government grants. Nonprofits in Davidson, Shelby, and Knox counties and 7th, 9th, and 2nd Congressional districts received the most government funding. Nonprofits in Benton, Sequatchie, and Pickett counties and 4th, 6th, and 1st Congressional districts would have the biggest gap to fill if they lost their government grants. Nonprofits in Benton, Campbell, and Cannon counties and 8th, 1st, and 9th Congressional districts have the highest share of nonprofits at risk of not covering their expenses if they lost their government grants.
